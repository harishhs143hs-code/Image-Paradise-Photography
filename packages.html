What affects the final photography quotation?

The final price depends on the type of session, event date, location, hours of coverage, number of events, photo/video requirements, album selection and travel. Destination bookings are planned individually, and travel/location costs are included in the final agreed quotation. Videography is quoted separately.

Photography services available to enquire about

  • Wedding, engagement and reception photography
  • Pre-wedding and couple photography
  • Baby, newborn, birthday and maternity photography
  • Family portraits and private celebrations
  • Corporate and other event photography
  • Wedding and event videography, quoted separately

Local and worldwide bookings

Our primary service region includes Veppampattu, Tiruvallur, Avadi, Ambattur, Chennai and nearby Tamil Nadu locations. Destination assignments elsewhere in India and overseas are available by prior arrangement, subject to availability and an agreed travel quotation.

How to request a quotation

  1. Share your name, event type, date and city/country.
  2. Tell us your expected coverage and whether you need photography, videography or an album.
  3. Share an approximate budget if you have one.
  4. We check availability and confirm the quotation and booking terms before a date is reserved.
