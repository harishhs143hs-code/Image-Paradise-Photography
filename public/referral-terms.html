Referral & Partner Program Terms

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This page describes the baseline terms for submitting a referral to Image Paradise Photography (“the studio”). It does not promise a particular reward amount. The studio and the partner must confirm the applicable commission and payout milestone in writing before a referral is accepted.

Who may refer

Individuals and professional partners may introduce new clients who are considering wedding photography, videography, pre-wedding shoots, engagement photography or related services. Business development arrangements may have additional written terms.

Eligible referrals

  • A referral should be submitted before the prospective client has already contacted the studio or booked the relevant service.
  • Provide accurate information and clearly identify the person making the referral.
  • Do not submit duplicate, fabricated, self-referred or unlawfully obtained leads.
  • Obtain permission before sharing another person's name or contact details.
  • The studio may ask for enough information to identify the referral and determine whether it is eligible.

Commission and payment

The commission amount or calculation, eligible package, currency, payout method and payout timing must be agreed with the studio in writing before the referral is accepted. No fixed amount, client discount or payout deadline is promised by this page. Unless the written agreement says otherwise, a referral reward is earned only after the client confirms the booking and the agreed client payment milestone has been received by the studio.

Cancelled, refunded, reversed, disputed or fraudulent bookings may not qualify. Applicable adjustments will be handled according to the written agreement and applicable law. The studio will request payment details only when a valid reward is due and will use them only to arrange the agreed payment and maintain necessary records.

Client decisions and responsibilities

The studio independently handles service descriptions, pricing, quotations, date availability, client communications, contracts, delivery and fulfilment. A referral partner must not promise an unconfirmed price, discount, reward, booking, availability or service guarantee on the studio's behalf.

Transparency and conflicts

Referral partners should disclose a relevant financial relationship when recommending the studio where required by applicable law or platform rules. The program is not employment, an agency appointment or authority to enter a contract on behalf of the studio.

Changes and questions

The studio may update the program for future referrals. Any specific commission already agreed in writing should be handled according to that agreement. Contact imageparadise2020@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 7708510143 before making a referral if you need confirmation.
