Privacy Notice

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Image Paradise Photography (“we”, “us” or “the studio”) uses this notice to explain how information submitted through our website is handled. Please review it before sending a form or referral.

Information you may provide

Depending on the form, you may provide your name, contact details, event date, venue or city, service interests, budget range, message, and referral information. Please do not send payment credentials or other sensitive information through a general enquiry form.

How we use information

  • Respond to photography, videography, package, date and callback enquiries.
  • Prepare quotations and coordinate a booking or service.
  • Record a referral, contact the referrer and determine eligibility under the written referral terms.
  • Protect forms from misuse and maintain business records.

Services used to process information

Website hosting and form/chat functions may involve service providers, including Vercel, Web3Forms, WhatsApp and the AI service used to answer website chat messages. Information sent to those providers may be processed under their own terms and privacy notices. A form may open WhatsApp so that you can send the details to the studio; review the message before sending it.

Sharing and consent

We do not sell personal information. We may share the minimum information needed with service providers operating website, communications or business functions, or where disclosure is required by law. Before submitting someone else's contact information as a referral, obtain their permission.

Retention and requests

We aim to retain enquiry and referral information only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage the business relationship, handle payments or disputes, and meet applicable legal obligations. To request access, correction or deletion of information, contact imageparadise2020@gmail.com. We may need to retain certain records where required for legitimate business or legal purposes.

Security and third-party websites

We use reasonable measures to protect information but cannot promise that any internet transmission or third-party service is completely secure. Links to external services are governed by those services' own privacy practices.

Updates and contact

We may update this notice when our forms or services change. For privacy questions, contact Image Paradise Photography at imageparadise2020@gmail.com or +91 7708510143.
