Photography Guide

Wedding photography terms, explained

Simple answers to common photography and videography terms.

01What is candid wedding photography?+
Candid wedding photography is photography that captures natural moments as they happen. It focuses on real emotions, family interactions and genuine expressions instead of only posed photographs.
02What is traditional wedding photography?+
Traditional wedding photography is a planned style that includes posed portraits, family photographs and important ceremony moments. It helps ensure that key people and rituals are documented clearly.
03What is cinematic wedding videography?+
Cinematic wedding videography is a storytelling style that combines meaningful moments, creative shots, music and editing to create a short wedding film. It is designed to capture the feeling and story of the celebration.
04What is a pre-wedding photoshoot?+
A pre-wedding photoshoot is a photography session before the wedding. Couples can choose locations, outfits and concepts that reflect their relationship and personal style.
05What is a wedding highlight video?+
A wedding highlight video is a short edited film that presents selected moments from the wedding day. It usually focuses on emotions, important events and memorable details.

PHOTOGRAPHY GUIDE

Wedding Photography Terms, Explained

What is candid wedding photography?

Candid wedding photography captures natural moments without asking people to pose for every photograph. It focuses on real emotions, interactions, laughter and meaningful details throughout the wedding.

What is traditional wedding photography?

Traditional wedding photography records important ceremonies, family portraits, group photographs and formal moments with planned compositions. It complements candid coverage by making sure key people and rituals are documented.

What is cinematic wedding videography?

Cinematic wedding videography presents your wedding as a story using carefully selected moments, movement, audio and edited sequences. A highlight film focuses on the emotion and atmosphere of the celebration.

What is a pre-wedding photoshoot?

A pre-wedding photoshoot is a photography session before the wedding. Couples can choose a location, concept and outfit style that reflects their relationship and personality.

How long does wedding photo delivery take?

A preview selection is typically shared within 24–48 hours. Full edited wedding galleries are generally delivered within 4–6 weeks, while album design and printing may require additional time.

How do I book a wedding photographer?

Start by sharing your name, WhatsApp number, event date, location and coverage requirements. The team can then check availability, discuss your needs and recommend a suitable package.
