Privacy policy

This page explains how the website's enquiry flow works and what to consider before sharing information.

Information you choose to share

The enquiry form on this website formats the details you enter into a message and opens WhatsApp. The form does not submit those details to a website database. The message is not sent until you choose to send it in WhatsApp.

Details may include your name, contact number, event type, event date, location and approximate budget. Please do not share passwords, payment-card details, identity documents or other sensitive information in an enquiry.

WhatsApp and third-party services

When you continue to WhatsApp, your use of that service and the information you send are governed by WhatsApp/Meta's own terms and privacy practices. Links to social networks and other external websites are also subject to their own policies.

Hosting and technical logs

The website is hosted on Vercel. Hosting providers may process routine technical information such as request details and security logs to deliver and protect the website. This page does not claim that the hosting provider retains no technical logs.

Your choices and questions

You can choose not to submit an enquiry. To ask how information you have already sent is being used, contact Image Paradise Photography through WhatsApp.
