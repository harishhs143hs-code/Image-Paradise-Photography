Privacy information

Privacy policy

This policy explains how Image Paradise Photography handles booking enquiries and referral programme information that you choose to share through the website and WhatsApp.

Information you choose to share

The enquiry form opens a prepared WhatsApp message and, when the booking database is configured, securely submits the details you consent to share to the Image Paradise Photography lead system. The WhatsApp message is not sent until you choose to send it in WhatsApp. If the database is temporarily unavailable, the form will tell you and you can still contact the team through WhatsApp.

Details may include your name, phone number, optional email, event type, event date and flexibility, event location, expected coverage, approximate budget and requirements. The details are used to respond to and manage your booking enquiry. Please do not share passwords, payment-card details, identity documents or other sensitive information in an enquiry.

Enquiry records and retention

When database storage is enabled, enquiry details are accessible to authorised Image Paradise Photography staff through a restricted lead dashboard. They are not intended for public display. The team should retain enquiries only as long as reasonably needed for customer communication, booking administration and applicable legal requirements, then securely delete or anonymise them.

Referral programme information

If you use the Refer & Earn form, it may prepare a WhatsApp message containing your name, phone number, city and participant profile, together with the prospective customer's name, phone number, event location and requested service. The message is not sent to Image Paradise until you review it and press Send in WhatsApp. You must obtain the prospective customer's permission before sharing their contact details or enquiry. Referral information is used to assess eligibility, communicate with the participant and administer any reward agreed in writing. Do not send contact lists or information about people who have not consented.

WhatsApp and third-party services

When you continue to WhatsApp, your use of that service and the information you send are governed by WhatsApp/Meta's own terms and privacy practices. Links to social networks and other external websites are also subject to their own policies.

Hosting and technical logs

The website is hosted on Vercel. Hosting providers may process routine technical information such as request details and security logs to deliver and protect the website. This page does not claim that the hosting provider retains no technical logs.

Your choices and questions

You can choose not to submit an enquiry. To ask how information you have already sent is being used, contact Image Paradise Photography through WhatsApp.
