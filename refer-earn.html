Referral Partner Agreement & Guidelines

Please read these terms before registering. This is a referral arrangement with Image Paradise Photography, not an employment offer.

  1. No fee to join: Image Paradise does not charge a registration, training, security or joining fee. Do not pay anyone claiming to represent this programme.
  2. No salary or guaranteed income: This is not a job, payroll role, franchise, investment or multi-level marketing scheme. There is no fixed salary, minimum earning, or guarantee that a referral will convert.
  3. Agree the reward first: For each eligible referral, Image Paradise will confirm the applicable reward amount or calculation and the payout condition in writing (for example, through its official WhatsApp) before the referral is treated as accepted. Do not assume a reward amount from an old post or another person's referral.
  4. Eligible new referrals: The client must be a genuine prospective customer introduced by you before they have already contacted Image Paradise or been recorded as an active enquiry. Existing customers, duplicate leads and enquiries already in progress may not qualify.
  5. Client permission and privacy: Obtain the client's permission before sharing their name, phone number, event details or other personal information. Never send contact lists or private data without consent. The client may contact us directly instead.
  6. Successful booking: A referral becomes reward-eligible only when Image Paradise confirms in writing that the referral is accepted, the customer books an eligible service, and the agreed payment milestone is met. A query, quotation or verbal promise alone is not a confirmed booking.
  7. Cancellation or refund: If a booking is cancelled, refunded or not paid as agreed, the reward may not become payable. If a reward was already paid and the booking is later fully refunded due to cancellation, both parties will discuss a fair adjustment in writing.
  8. Honest promotion: Do not promise prices, discounts, availability, deliverables, service quality or rewards on behalf of Image Paradise. Do not use misleading job advertisements, spam, fake enquiries, impersonation or pressure tactics.
  9. Independent participation: You choose whether and when to share referrals. You are not an employee or authorised agent and cannot sign contracts, collect customer payments or make commitments on behalf of Image Paradise.
  10. Reward payment: The payment method, expected payout date and any applicable documentation or statutory tax treatment will be confirmed in writing for each accepted referral before the booking is treated as reward-eligible.
  11. Fair administration: Image Paradise may reject duplicate, incomplete, fraudulent or previously known enquiries and may update or pause this programme for future referrals. Any change will not cancel a reward already confirmed in writing for an accepted referral, except as allowed under the agreed cancellation terms.
  12. Questions or disputes: Contact Image Paradise at +91 77085 10143 on WhatsApp. Keep the written referral and reward confirmation for your records.

Frequently asked questions

Understand the referral opportunity before joining.

Is this a work-from-home job?

It is a flexible referral partner opportunity that you can participate in remotely. It is not salaried employment and does not provide a fixed monthly income.

How much can I earn per referral?

The reward depends on the final service/package and is confirmed with you in writing before an eligible referral is accepted. There is no universal fixed amount or guaranteed earning.

Do I need to pay to join?

No. There is no joining, registration, training or security fee. Do not pay anyone who asks for money to enrol you.

When will I receive the reward?

The payout condition, method and expected date are confirmed in writing for each accepted referral. A reward is not due merely because a person asks for a quote.

Can I refer someone outside Chennai?

You may introduce enquiries from other locations. Whether Image Paradise can serve the event depends on location, availability and the agreed quotation.
