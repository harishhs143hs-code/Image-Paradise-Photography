About the photography team

Photography rooted in Tamil Nadu. Available for destination bookings worldwide.

Image Paradise Photography is an independent photography and videography service based in Veppampattu near Avadi, in the Tiruvallur region of Tamil Nadu, India. We serve customers across Chennai, Tiruvallur, Avadi, Ambattur and nearby locations, and accept on-location assignments elsewhere in India and internationally by prior arrangement.

We photograph weddings and engagements, pre-wedding sessions, baby and newborn milestones, birthdays, maternity sessions, family portraits and events. Our portfolio is the best way to understand the moments, people and visual style we work to capture. We encourage customers to review the photographs and videos, discuss the coverage they need and ask questions before making a booking.

A clear booking experience

Every project starts with the event type, date, location, coverage hours and the deliverables you have in mind. We check availability and prepare a quotation around those requirements. Photography and videography are scoped separately, so the customer can understand what is included. Qualifying photography sessions start at ₹8,000 for up to 3 hours; the final quote may vary with event scope, location and travel.

For printed wedding albums, customers can select 100 photographs for an ₹8,000 album option or 200 photographs for a ₹15,000 option. Album specifications, approvals, delivery timelines and international shipping arrangements are confirmed before production.

Where we work

Our primary service region includes Veppampattu, Tiruvallur, Avadi, Ambattur, Chennai and nearby Tamil Nadu locations. We also consider destination assignments in other Indian states and overseas locations. Worldwide travel is not an automatic same-day service: each destination booking is subject to availability, advance planning and an agreed travel quotation.
