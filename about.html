About the studio

Photography for the moments that matter

Image Paradise Photography is a wedding and portrait photography service based in Veppampattu near Avadi, serving Chennai, Tiruvallur and Ambattur.

Our work covers candid wedding photography, traditional wedding photography, wedding videography, engagement sessions, pre-wedding shoots, bridal portraits and album design. The goal is to document the people, rituals and emotions that make each celebration personal.

The team plans coverage around your event date, venue, schedule and preferred style. Share your requirements to discuss availability and the right photo/video coverage for your celebration.

Where we work

We serve Chennai, Avadi, Veppampattu, Tiruvallur, Ambattur and nearby areas. Travel to other locations can be discussed when arranging the booking.
