Wedding Film Deliverables

Every video package includes carefully crafted deliverables designed for sharing and preservation:

  • Highlight Film — A beautifully edited summary of your wedding day featuring key moments set to music. Perfect for sharing with friends and family.
  • Feature Film — An extended edit covering all major events in chronological order, typically 20-40 minutes.
  • Teaser Video — A quick 30-60 second edit designed for social media sharing immediately after your wedding.
  • Ceremony Coverage — Full documentation of religious ceremonies including vows, rituals, and blessings.
  • Social Media Cuts — Vertical and horizontal formats optimized for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and WhatsApp sharing.

Wedding Videography vs Wedding Photography

Photography and videography serve complementary purposes. We recommend investing in both for complete documentation of your wedding day.

  • Photography captures perfect moments—expressions frozen in time, beautiful compositions you can frame and display.
  • Videography captures living memories—the movement, sound, and emotion that photos cannot convey.
  • Together, they create a complete archive of your celebration, each medium enhancing the other.

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes! Our videographers and photographers work as a coordinated team, ensuring we capture every moment without interfering with each other's shots.

Absolutely! We encourage couples to share their favorite songs. We'll help select licensed music that fits your vision and edit the film to match your chosen tracks.
