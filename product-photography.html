Product photography

Clear, considered product images help customers understand what you offer. Tell us about your products, where the images will be used, and the look you have in mind so we can discuss a suitable shoot plan.

Plan a product photography session

Every product shoot has different requirements. Share the product category, number of items, preferred background or setting, image orientation, and whether you need detail shots or a consistent set across a collection. We’ll review the brief and confirm what can be covered in a written quotation.

What to include in your enquiry

  • Product type and approximate number of items
  • Where the photographs will be used, such as an online store, catalogue or social media
  • Preferred style, background, framing and any reference examples
  • Shoot location and preferred date or timeframe
  • Required image quantity, formats and any retouching expectations

Quotation and next steps

Pricing and availability are confirmed after we review the products, shoot location, image requirements and deliverables. Please do not assume a standard package or turnaround time; these details will be stated in your quote before you confirm.
