Tiruvallur Wedding Traditions

Tiruvallur district is known for its rich cultural heritage and traditional Tamil Hindu weddings. Having photographed numerous weddings in Tiruvallur, we've become adept at anticipating and capturing the key moments of traditional ceremonies including:

  • Nichayathartham — Engagement ceremonies with ritual exchange of gifts
  • Kanyadaanam — The emotional moment when the father gives his daughter away
  • Mangalsutra Dharanam — The tying of the sacred thread
  • Saptapadi — The seven steps around the sacred fire
  • Graha Pravesham — The bride's first entry into her new home

We understand the significance of each ritual and ensure comprehensive coverage without disrupting the sacred proceedings.
